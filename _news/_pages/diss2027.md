## Welcome to DiSS 2027! 

Welcome to the 13th edition of the DiSS workshop, which will be held on February 4th-5th, in Bolzano-Bozen, Italy! 

Join researchers from various research fields and perspectives to explore the study of disfluencies in spontaneous speech in the winter setting of the Dolomites.

<div class="text-center">
{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/Bz2pro.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded" width="100%" %}
</div>

Submissions are encouraged from all fields that deal with disfluency, paralinguistics, and other related phenomena. This includes, among others: psychology, neuropsychology and neurocognition, psycholinguistics, linguistics, conversation analysis, computational linguistics, speech technology, gesture analysis, dialogue systems, speech production and perception. 

We are looking forward to seeing you in Bozen!
