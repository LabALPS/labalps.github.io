## Welcome!


Welcome to the 13th (winter) edition of the Workshop on Disfluency in Spontaneous Speech!


Join researchers from various research fields and perspectives to explore the study of disfluencies in spontaneous speech in the snowy setting of the Dolomites.


We are looking forward to seeing you in Bozen!
