## Welcome to DiSS 2027!

<br>

For the first time, DiSS goes winter. The 13th edition of the workshop will be held on February 4th–5th in Bolzano-Bozen, Italy!

<br>

Join researchers from various research fields and perspectives to explore the study of disfluencies in spontaneous speech in the winter setting of the Dolomites.
