The **Alpine Laboratory of Phonetic Sciences (ALPS)** is a research group focused on **phonetics** and **speech technology**.  
We investigate how speech sounds are produced and perceived, and we build tools for **automatic speech recognition (ASR)**, **forced alignment** for both standard and **low-resource** languages and dialects.

Our work spans experimental phonetics (acoustic analysis, perceptual studies), computational modeling (deep neural networks for ASR/TTS), and resources for minority languages. We collaborate with universities and research centers to release **open datasets**, **reproducible code**, and **interactive demos** whenever possible.

If you’re a prospective student or collaborator, check our **[open topics](/projects/)** and feel free to **[get in touch](mailto:alplabphon@gmail.com)**.  
We welcome visitors and are happy to discuss joint projects on phonetics, dialectology, and speech technologies for under-resourced varieties.

*ALPS is committed to open science. Unless restricted, we share code and datasets to foster replicability and real-world impact in speech research.*
